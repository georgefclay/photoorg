Estimate when this photograph was taken, from visual evidence only.

Look at: clothing and hemlines, hairstyles, glasses frames, car models and shapes,
appliances and furniture, print border style, paper finish, film grain, colour cast
and fading, black-and-white versus colour, image sharpness, and any visible signage
or packaging.

Rules:
- Always answer with a RANGE of years, never a single year.
- The range must span at least 3 years. If you are unsure, widen it to at least 10
  years. It is far better to be wide and right than narrow and wrong.
- year_min must be less than or equal to year_max.
- confidence is 0 to 1 and describes how sure you are the true date falls inside
  your range.
- reasoning: one or two sentences naming the specific visual evidence you used.
  Name the things you actually saw. Do not mention people by name.
- is_scan_of_print: true if this looks like a scan of a physical print — a white or
  patterned print border, rounded corners, paper texture, a date stamp printed by a
  photo lab, or scanner artefacts. False for a native digital photograph.

Respond with a single JSON object and nothing else. No markdown, no code fences,
no commentary before or after.

{"year_min": <integer year>, "year_max": <integer year>,
 "confidence": <number between 0 and 1>,
 "reasoning": "<one or two sentences of visual evidence>",
 "is_scan_of_print": <true or false>}
