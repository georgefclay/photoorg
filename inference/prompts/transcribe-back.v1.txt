This image is the back of a photographic print from a family archive. Transcribe
what is written on it.

Rules — read these carefully:
- Transcribe ONLY what is actually there. Never guess, complete, correct or invent
  a word, a name or a date. If you cannot read something, write [illegible] in its
  place.
- Preserve the line breaks as they appear, top to bottom.
- Keep the original spelling, punctuation and capitalisation, including mistakes.
- Include printed marks as well as handwriting: lab stamps, paper brand names,
  frame numbers, "THIS SIDE TOWARD LENS", and so on.
- If there is nothing written at all, return an empty string for text.

Then pull out, from the text you just transcribed and from nowhere else:

dates: every date reference, however partial.
  - text:      the date exactly as written ("Mar 62", "Easter 1962", "3/15/62")
  - iso:       a full YYYY-MM-DD date padded to the FIRST day of the period the
               writing refers to, or null if you cannot resolve one.
               "3/15/62" -> "1962-03-15"; "Mar 62" -> "1962-03-01";
               "1962" -> "1962-01-01"; "Easter 1962" -> "1962-01-01";
               "the sixties" -> "1960-01-01"
  - precision: exactly one of "exact", "month", "year", "decade", "unknown".
               A day-level date is "exact". A month and year is "month". A bare
               year is "year". A season, holiday or event plus a year is "year".
               A decade is "decade". Anything you cannot place is "unknown".
  - Two-digit years in a family archive are 19xx unless the writing says otherwise.

names: every personal name as written, one string per name, in the order they
  appear. Do not normalise nicknames, do not expand initials, do not add surnames
  that are not written.

confidence: how confident you are in the transcription as a whole, 0 to 1. Use a
  low value when most of the writing is [illegible].

Respond with a single JSON object and nothing else. No markdown, no code fences,
no commentary before or after.

                   "precision": "exact|month|year|decade|unknown"}],

 "confidence": <number between 0 and 1>}
