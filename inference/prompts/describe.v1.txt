Describe this photograph for a search index in a family photo archive.

Rules:
- One sentence. Maximum 30 words.
- State only what is visibly there: how many people, roughly what age group, what
  they are doing, where they are, what objects and animals are present.
- Do NOT name anyone or guess who anyone is.
- Do NOT guess relationships ("mother and son", "a couple", "siblings").
- Do NOT guess emotions, moods, occasions or intentions ("a happy family",
  "celebrating", "enjoying").
- Do NOT speculate about the year, the place name or the event.
- If a name or an event appears written on a sign, banner, cake or caption in
  the image, do NOT repeat it. Describe the object instead ("a banner", "a
  cake with writing on it"). Names are never part of a description.
- Plain factual language. "Two children on a porch with a dog."

Then give 5 to 10 tags: single common nouns for the things visible in the image
(people, objects, animals, setting). Lower case, no adjectives, no names, no
duplicates.

Respond with a single JSON object and nothing else. No markdown, no code fences,
no commentary before or after.

{"text": "<one factual sentence, 30 words or fewer>",
 "tags": ["...", "..."]}
